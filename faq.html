Frequently Asked

Straight answers.

Do you install, or is it supply only?

Fencly is supply only. We supply WPC composite fencing, cladding and gates. We do not install, but we're happy to recommend trusted local installers if needed — just tick the box on the enquiry form.

How much do WPC fence panels cost?

Supply only, inclusive of GST: 1.8m — $270 per panel, 2.1m — $320 per panel, 2.5m — $350 per panel. Styles are Semi Trellis, Full Closed (Privacy) and Full Open (Trellis). Prices exclude delivery. See the full price list →

How much does WPC cladding cost?

2.3m boards in Charcoal Black or Smoke Grey are $30 per board. The premium 2.7m Teak/Black combination board is $37 per board. Both inclusive of GST, supply only, plus delivery.

What do gates cost?

Supply only, inclusive of GST: Pedestrian Gate (1000 × 1200mm) $599; Single Swing Gate (1000 × 1800mm) $649; Double Swing Gate (2700 × 1800mm) $1,500; Sliding Gate 4.5m $3,960; Sliding Gate 5.0m $4,400. Sliding gates are priced at $800 per metre plus GST ($880 per metre inc GST), motor included.

What colours can I get?

WPC fence panels and gates come in Charcoal Black, Smoke Grey, Light Grey, Monument, Teak and Redwood. Cladding comes in Charcoal Black and Smoke Grey at 2.3m, plus a Teak/Black combination at 2.7m. See the colour range →

What stock do you hold, and what are the lead times?

Teak is currently low stock. Monument, Light Grey and Redwood may have a lead time. We confirm stock and lead times for your exact colour and quantity when you enquire, before you commit to anything.

Do you deliver, or can I pick up?

Both. All prices are supply only plus delivery, or you can pick up free from our warehouse at Unit A, 107 Asquith St, Silverwater NSW 2128 by appointment. Delivery cost and timeframe are confirmed with your quote — tell us your postcode and we'll price it.

What warranty do I get?

All panels and boards carry a written 15-year warranty and need zero maintenance — no painting, staining, oiling or sealing, ever. Read the warranty terms →

Can you recommend an installer?

Yes. We don't install, but we work alongside installers across Sydney and are happy to point you at trusted local trades. Tick “Want an installer referral?” on the enquiry form and we'll include names with your quote. Prefer to do it yourself? See our installation guide.

Why co-extruded WPC rather than standard WPC or timber?

Standard (uncapped) WPC absorbs moisture and fades within 3–5 years. Co-extruded WPC bonds a sealed polymer skin around the entire core during manufacture, so the wood fibre is never exposed — the look of timber with none of the maintenance. How co-extrusion works →
