Why Sydney Homeowners Choose Fencly

Signature feature

Co-extruded, capped for life

A co-extruded ASA/PE skin wraps all four faces of every board. ASA — the weatherable polymer used on exterior automotive trim — holds colour and resists chalking far longer than the budget PVC caps on cheaper composite.

Handles Sydney sun

UV stabilisers and inorganic pigments shrug off harsh Australian sun with minimal fade — no greying, no annual re-oiling, no bleaching like timber paling.

Coastal & poolside ready

With the core fully sealed, salt air and chlorine can't reach the wood fibre — ideal for Northern Beaches boundaries and pool surrounds across Sydney.

Termite resistant

High polymer content and a sealed cap give termites and borers nothing to feed on — a real edge over hardwood and pine in Sydney's termite zones.

Sydney WPC FAQ

WPC fencing in Sydney — your questions.

How much does WPC fencing cost in Sydney?

Supplied and installed, WPC fencing in Sydney typically runs from about $250 to $380 per linear metre depending on height, colour, gates and site access. Fencly privacy panels start at $250, and every quote is a fixed price inclusive of GST after a free on-site measure. See the full cost breakdown →

Which Sydney suburbs do you cover?

We supply and install across Greater Sydney — including Parramatta, The Hills, the Northern Beaches, Liverpool, Blacktown, the Inner West and the Sutherland Shire — from our Silverwater (NSW 2128) warehouse. We also ship supply-only kits Australia-wide for DIY and trade.

Is WPC suitable for coastal and poolside sites?

Yes. The co-extruded cap seals the core against salt air, chlorine and damp, so the boards won't rot, swell or corrode — a strong fit for Northern Beaches boundaries and pool surrounds where timber greys and steel rusts.

How long does installation take?

Most residential boundaries are supplied and installed within a fortnight. Kits dispatch from Silverwater usually within a week, and the on-site install of posts, footings and boards generally takes one to three days depending on the run and access.
