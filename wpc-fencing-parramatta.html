Local Angle

Why Parramatta homeowners choose WPC.

Western Sydney's climate is unforgiving on fences. While the coast enjoys a sea breeze, Parramatta, Harris Park and Merrylands routinely bake past 40°C in summer, then cop dry heat and dust for weeks on end. Old timber paling fences across the district's established homes tell the story — silvered, cupped, split at the rails and leaning after a decade of that cycle. WPC was engineered for exactly this: the co-extruded ASA cap carries UV stabilisers and inorganic pigments that resist fade and chalking, and the polymer-rich board simply doesn't dry out, crack or warp the way hardwood and pine do.

Parramatta is also a suburb of upgrades. Post-war brick homes and inter-war cottages across North Parramatta and Granville are being renovated street by street, and a tired paling boundary undoes an otherwise sharp reno. Swapping to a capped-composite privacy fence in teak or charcoal instantly lifts the frontage — and unlike a repaint, you never have to touch it again.

The clincher is speed. Our warehouse sits at Unit A, 107 Asquith St, Silverwater — roughly 10 minutes from central Parramatta. That short haul means kits load quickly and our crews reach your site without a cross-city trek, so Parramatta jobs typically turn around faster than the Sydney average.
