Local Angle

Why Blacktown homeowners choose WPC.

Blacktown families are value-conscious and practical — nobody wants to pay for the same fence twice. But timber paling, the default across Seven Hills, Marayong and Doonside, is a false economy out here. Western Sydney's soils are termite-prone, and hollowed-out posts and chewed rails are a familiar sight the moment you lean on an older fence. Add the hot, dry summers that grey and split the boards, and a paling boundary is usually on borrowed time within a decade.

WPC changes the maths. It costs more up front than pine paling, but the whole-of-life picture is what matters: no repainting or re-oiling, no rot at the ground line, and — critically for this area — nothing for termites to eat. The high polymer content and sealed cap mean borers simply move on. Over the years you'd otherwise spend replacing and patching timber, a capped-composite fence quietly pays for itself while still looking like fresh timber.

It's why the most common job we do in Blacktown is a straight swap: pull out the old rotted or termite-eaten paling, set new posts, and install WPC panels as a clean, permanent boundary upgrade — one fixed price, inclusive of GST.

Blacktown WPC FAQ

WPC fencing in Blacktown — your questions.

Do you install in Blacktown?

Yes — Blacktown and the western suburbs are a core service area. We supply and install across Seven Hills, Kings Langley, Quakers Hill, Marayong and Doonside.

Is WPC really termite-proof?

Yes. Blacktown's soils are termite-prone, and timber posts and palings are a common casualty. WPC's high polymer content and fully sealed cap give termites and borers nothing to feed on, so the boards won't be eaten out the way pine and hardwood are.

Is it good value versus re-doing timber?

Yes. Timber paling is cheap up front but gets replaced every 10 to 15 years as it rots, greys and falls to termites. WPC costs more initially but is a once-and-done boundary with a 15-year warranty — over the life of the fence, it's the value choice. See the cost breakdown →

Can you replace my old rotted timber fence?

Absolutely — replacing tired, rotted or termite-damaged paling is one of our most common Blacktown jobs. We remove the old fence, set new posts and footings, and install capped WPC panels as a straight boundary upgrade, all at a fixed price inclusive of GST.
