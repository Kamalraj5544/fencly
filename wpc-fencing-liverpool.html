Local Angle

Why Liverpool homeowners choose WPC.

South West Sydney is the fastest-growing residential corridor in the city. Around Edmondson Park, Middleton Grange, Austral and Leppington, families are moving into fresh builds on tight blocks where the boundary is the only thing between one living room and the next. Zero-gap WPC privacy panels do what a paling fence can't: no shrinkage gaps opening up in year two, no neighbour's floodlight coming through.

Then there's the heat. Liverpool runs several degrees hotter than coastal Sydney on a summer afternoon, and fences wear it. Hardwood palings grey off, cup and split; a re-oil or repaint buys a couple of seasons before you're back at it. Colorbond holds up structurally but gets too hot to touch and shows every dent. Co-extruded WPC answers both — the sealed cap means UV and moisture never reach the wood fibre, so the boards keep their colour without oiling, staining or painting.

New estates also mean new pools. WPC screening handles chlorine splash and poolside damp without rotting like timber or rusting like steel, and the same system carries through to your boundary runs and gates — one supplier, one measure, one warranty across the whole yard.

Stock for all of it runs out of our Silverwater warehouse, a straight run up the M7, so there's no interstate freight leg to price in and no waiting on a container to land.
