## 2. Review generation — target 25+ reviews in 60 days

Goal: pass Heriton's 46 reviews and beat their 4.1★. Even 20–30 genuine 5★ reviews with the words "WPC fence" / "Sydney" in them will move rankings and click-through.

**Process:**
- [ ] Create your GBP **review short link** (from the GBP dashboard → "Get more reviews"). It looks like `https://g.page/r/XXXX/review`.
- [ ] Ask **every** completed customer, at handover while they're happy. In person > SMS > email for conversion.
- [ ] Send a follow-up **SMS within 24h** of completion (templates below).
- [ ] Email the last 6–12 months of past customers a one-off "how did we do?" request.
- [ ] **Respond to every review** (good and bad) within 48h — Google rewards engagement. Work "WPC fencing" / suburb names naturally into replies.
- [ ] Never buy or incentivise reviews (against Google policy and risks a penalty).

**SMS template:**
