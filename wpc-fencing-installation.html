Why Co-Extrusion

Timber looks. Engineered to never rot.

Standard WPC leaves the wood-fibre core exposed at the surface, so it still soaks up water and fades. Fencly's boards are co-extruded — a polymer cap is fused around the entire core as it's manufactured, sealing it for life.

  • Sealed against UV, water, salt air and stains
  • No painting, staining, oiling or sealing — ever
  • Termite-proof, rot-proof and pool-safe
  • 15-year written warranty on every panel

The Benefits

Built to outperform timber, vinyl & uncapped composite.

Signature feature

ASA / PE cap layers

A co-extruded ASA/PE skin wraps all four faces of the board. ASA — the same weatherable polymer used on exterior automotive trim — holds colour and resists chalking far longer than the cheap PVC caps on budget composite.

UV resistance

UV stabilisers and inorganic pigments shrug off harsh Australian sun with minimal fade — no greying, no annual re-oiling, no bleaching like timber.

Moisture & rot proof

With the core fully sealed, water can't reach the wood fibre — no swelling, no rot, no splitting. Ideal for pools, coastal boundaries and damp shaded sides.

Termite & pest resistant

High polymer content and a sealed cap give termites and borers nothing to feed on — a real edge over hardwood and pine paling in Sydney's termite zones.

Zero maintenance

No painting, staining, oiling or sealing — ever. An occasional hose-down keeps it new; the capped surface resists mould, mildew and stains.

15-year warranty

Every panel ships with a written 15-year limited warranty against splitting, cracking, rotting, peeling and termite damage. Read the terms →

Anatomy of the Board

A capped core, sealed on every face.

  1. 01 — Outer

    ASA / PE cap

    Weatherable, UV-stabilised polymer skin. Holds colour, resists scratches, stains and chalking.

  2. 02 — Bond

    Co-extrusion fusion

    Cap and core are extruded together, so the skin is molecularly bonded — it can't peel or delaminate.

  3. 03 — Core

    HDPE + wood-fibre core

    Dense recycled timber fibre and high-density polymer for a rigid, timber-like board that won't rot.

WPC FAQ

Premium WPC fencing questions.

What are ASA and PE cap layers?

They're the protective outer skin co-extruded around the board. PE (polyethylene) seals the core against moisture; ASA (acrylonitrile styrene acrylate) adds superior UV and colour stability — it's the same class of weatherable polymer used on exterior automotive panels. Together they keep the board looking new without paint or stain.

How much does premium WPC fencing cost?

Upfront it sits roughly 25–40% above hardwood paling and Colorbond, but over the life of the fence — with no staining, painting or board replacement — it typically costs less than timber. Every Fencly quote is a fixed price inclusive of GST. Book a free measure for figures on your boundary.

Do you install, or is it supply only?

Both. We offer full supply-and-install across Greater Sydney, and supply-only kits for DIY and trade customers Australia-wide, dispatched from our Prestons warehouse.

Is WPC suitable for pools and coastal sites?

Yes. Because the core is fully sealed, the boards resist chlorine, salt air and constant damp without rotting, swelling or corroding — making WPC an excellent choice for pool surrounds and beachside boundaries.
