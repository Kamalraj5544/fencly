Estate Building

Cladding on a project-home budget

South West Sydney is building faster than anywhere else in the city. Edmondson Park, Middleton Grange, Austral and Leppington are turning over new lots continuously, and almost every project home on them comes with a facade package where the timber-look element is an upgrade line item.

That upgrade is usually the first thing owners look at replacing, because the standard offering is either painted fibre cement pretending to be timber or a small run of real hardwood that needs oiling from year two. Priced by the board rather than by the square metre, capped WPC lets you cost the actual wall — count the boards, multiply, done — and compare it honestly against what the builder quoted.

It also matters that the number doesn't move later. There's no staining cycle, no re-coat, no board replacement at year eight. What you pay for the boards is what the wall costs over its life.

Narrow Lots

Side passages, zero-lot walls and heat

The other thing about the new estates is how close the houses sit. Zero-lot and near-zero-lot builds leave side passages you can barely turn around in, and a rendered wall a metre from the neighbour's is a permanent maintenance headache — you can't get a ladder in to repaint it properly.

Cladding those walls in capped board is a one-time job. Nothing needs re-coating, so nobody has to get back in there. The same logic covers zero-lot boundary walls and the blank garage elevations that face the street on narrow frontages.

Heat is the last factor. South West Sydney runs hotter than the coast — 40°C-plus stretches are normal through summer. UV stabilisers and inorganic pigments under the cap are what keep the board from chalking and fading through that, which cheap uncapped WPC will do inside three to five years.

Liverpool Cladding FAQ

Questions we get from Liverpool.

How much is WPC cladding in Liverpool?

Supply only, inclusive of GST: $30 per 2.3m board in Charcoal Black or Smoke Grey, and $37 per 2.7m board in the Teak/Black combination. Pricing is per board rather than per square metre, so you can count the wall and cost it exactly. Delivery is quoted separately.

Is it cheaper than the builder's facade upgrade?

It depends what the builder quoted, but pricing by the board makes it easy to check — count the boards the wall needs, multiply by $30 or $37, and compare. The other half of the comparison is that there's no oiling or re-coating cost afterwards, which a hardwood batten upgrade will carry from about year two.

How fast can you get boards to a South West Sydney site?

Quickly. Orders are packed at our Silverwater warehouse and run straight down the M7, so there's no interstate freight and no consolidation wait. We confirm the exact lead time with your quote, along with current stock on your colour.

Will it fade in South West Sydney summers?

Not the capped board. UV stabilisers and inorganic pigments sit under a co-extruded cap, which is what holds colour through the 40°C-plus runs. Cheap uncapped WPC is the one that chalks and fades within three to five years — we only supply co-extruded, capped board.

Do you install cladding in Liverpool?

No — Fencly is supply only. We supply the boards and can recommend trusted local installers working across Liverpool, Casula, Prestons and the newer estates. Ask for a referral with your enquiry.
